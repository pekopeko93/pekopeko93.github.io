Hi, I'm Kumi 🌸

I'm a native Japanese speaker from Tottori, and an online Japanese teacher. I also run the YouTube channel peko peko vlog, where I talk about everyday life, feelings, and culture in real, natural Japanese.

Many of my students already know a lot of Japanese, but still feel that they sound "like a textbook." That's exactly where I love to help — turning correct Japanese into natural Japanese, the way people really talk. With advanced learners, we dive into the subtle nuances between words and expressions that even textbooks can't fully explain.

My Story

Why I teach natural Japanese

I started my YouTube channel to share the Japanese that textbooks leave out — how we really talk about our day, our worries, and our feelings. My first podcast episode was about my hometown, Tottori, and to my surprise it became my most-watched video.

Today, learners from Japan, Korea, Vietnam, the US, Taiwan and many other places listen along. Every video has Japanese subtitles with furigana, so you can read along while you train your ears.

In my lessons, I bring that same feeling: honest, warm conversation in real Japanese.
